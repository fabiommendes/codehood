How it works

Three steps, and you're teaching.

1

Scaffold your course

codehood init creates a course/ folder, a config file plus directories for questions, exams, calendar, and resources. All plain text, all yours to version.

2

Write content your way

Craft questions, exams, and resources locally, in whatever editor and workflow you already use. No forms, no lock-in.

3

Push, and it's live

codehood push syncs your changes to the server. Students see updates in their browser immediately, no separate publish step.

Students just open a browser.

No CLI required. Students use Codehood like a regular web-based LMS, checking the calendar, taking exams, and working through questions their instructor has published. The CLI is there if they want it, never if they don't.
